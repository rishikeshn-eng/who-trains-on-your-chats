App by app

This covers the personal versions of each app, free and paid. Every line is backed by a sentence from the company's own policy. Open "Their exact words" to read it.

Turn it off

Where each company says the setting is. Menus move, so if you can't find it, search the app's settings for the words in quotes.

Policy changes

Every day a script re-reads each policy page. If the page changes, or one of the quoted sentences disappears, it is listed here.

How this works

Method

  • I read each company's own privacy and help pages and picked the sentences that answer five questions: do they train on chats, where is the off switch, how long are chats kept, is there a private mode, and what is the catch.
  • A script checks every day that each sentence is still on the page, word for word. A green "still on the page" means it was found today.
  • If a sentence is gone, the entry is marked in red until I re-read the policy and update it.

Limits

  • A policy says what a company may do. This page can't see what any company actually does with your chats.
  • "Unless you turn it off" is my reading of each quote. Read the exact words and judge for yourself.
  • It covers personal accounts only. Work, school and developer accounts usually have different rules.
  • Rules can differ by country. These are the English pages as served to my location.
  • This is information, and it isn't legal advice.
